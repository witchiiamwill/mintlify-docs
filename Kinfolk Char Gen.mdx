## Merits and Flaws

We allow players to purchase as many merits as they can afford within reason that makes sense for the character and up to 7 points max in flaws. We have removed the original cap of 7 points max merits at Chargen. Just because they can take it doesn't mean they should. Its okay to tell people no if it's a balancing concern. Please consult your Head ST if you have any questions! \
\
Merits may be purchased at cost x 3.\
\
**Flaws may be purchased off with appropriate documented roleplay at flaw level x 3.**
