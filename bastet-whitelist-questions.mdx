---
title: "Bastet Whitelist Questions"
---

```text
1) How do the Bastet believe the world came to be? Who is Gaia in the eyes of the Bastet?

2) What do the Bastet call the Triat? How does their belief of the Triat differ from the other changing breeds?

3) What is The War of Rage? What was Bastet's role in it?

4) Name the nine tribes of the Bastets and the types of cats those tribes are associated with. Describe at least three in great detail.

5) Which tribe is your Bastet from? What drew you to play this tribe specifically? What sets this tribe apart from the others?

6.) How is your tribe organized? 

7.) How does your tribe view their kinfolk?

8.) How do the Bastet view the rest of the magical world? What is unique about this perspective?

9.) What is a Pryio? Explain the affect a Pryio can have on a Bastet.

10.) What is the Bastet Litany called? What are its tenants? How does it differ from the Garou litany?

11.) Explain how the Bastet hierarchy works. What names do they have of their ranks and what do they mean?

12.) Describe what a first change would look like for a Bastet.

13.) Can Bastet have warborn children? If so, what is the consequence of doing so?

14.) LSWOD allows all nine tribes of the Bastet to be playable in game. However, due to the diverse and expansive nature of the types of big cats the Bastet can be, getting specific peds to represent all nine tribes is a slow and ongoing process. Do you understand that you will likely need to utilize statuses, /mes, ooc communication and theatre of the mind often to explain that your ped’s appearance doesn’t match what they actually are?
```
