## Current limits:

Elders and political heads (Barons, Tower members, etc): up to 2 ghouls \
All other Kindred: 1 ghoul \
All ghouls should be approved by the Baron overseeing the territory in which that character primarily operates if it is mentioned in their barony rules. 

## Responsibility & PK Clause

A ghoul’s existence is directly tied to their domitor. The perma clause of the sire/domitor extends to their ghoul. This also applies to individuals who have been taught dangerous out-of-Clan Disciplines or similarly sensitive knowledge. TL;DR: if the domitor dies, the ghoul’s life may also come to an end in most cases (though final outcomes will depend on the roleplay). Likewise, if a ghoul is placed in an exceptionally dangerous or violent situation, that may also result in permadeath, depending on what they experience. 

## Transfers & Ownership

Ghouls may be taken as punishment by Elders or Barons, but they are not commodities and should not be traded casually. Relinquishing a ghoul should only happen under rare, logical, and compelling circumstances (for example, immediate danger to the domitor and recognition that they can no longer protect or maintain the bond). Be aware: a ghoul being taken or relinquished reflects poorly on the Kindred involved. It signals weakness, poor judgment, or failure, and others will perceive it accordingly (often depending on Clan culture and expectations). 

## Training & Eligibility

Training on proper ghouling and domitor conduct may be provided by Elders or experienced Kindred. Not every Kindred is immediately suited to take a ghoul. Age, experience, and Clan expectations matter. 

## Pacing

 Ghouling should be handled as a slow-burn process. Allow one in-game month (1 IRL week) between each vitae intake. This is to preserve narrative weight and avoid rushed bonding arcs. Keep in mind that fast paced ghouling has a high risk of causing overdose for the ghoul! 

## Story Teller Oversight

 Please open a ticket to catalogue your ghouling process with the Storyteller Team, including any relevant training or major developments. We strongly encourage players to discuss boundaries OOC beforehand. Consent is paramount! Ghouling can be an intense and personal storyline, and it should be handled with care by everyone involved. Ghouling carries significant responsibility, as another player is entrusting you with their character. Their failures are your failures, just as yours are theirs. Repeated mistakes may result in your ghoul being handed to your Elders, or killed outright if your Clan has no Elders, they refuse them, or they have already reached their limit. Bonds should also be treated as a case by case situation depending on the Clans, Sects, characters... Thus why we again really highly encourage players to set clear boundaries and give their expectations beforehand.
