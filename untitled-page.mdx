ROKEA WL V 1.1

Rokea WL Questions

1) According to the Rokea, what is the trait? How does their belief of the Triat differ from that of the Garou’s?

3) What is The Wound? How did The Wound come to be according to the Rokea?

4) What are the Rokea auspices? Explain them in detail.

5) Describe what happens during a Rokea’s first change. What happens after?

6) What are the names of the various forms the Rokea can take? What do they look like?

7) What is The Sending and why is it significant to the Rokea?

8) What is the Rokea Litany called? What are its tenants?

9) Explain the Rokea ranking system. How do Rokea earn honor and renown?

10) What is a Betweener? How do the Rokea view the Betweeners?

11) What is the War of Rage and what role did the Rokea play in it?

12) What are the Eastern Rokea called? What are some of the differences between them and their western counterparts?
