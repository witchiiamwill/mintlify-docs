---
title: "Perma Requests"
---

In the World of Darkness, actions have reactions and sometimes, those reactions are dire consequences.

If a player discovers that you have done certain things, are certain things, or have caused them or those they care about enough turmoil to warrant wanting you dead, they may open a Storyteller Ticket requesting approval for a permanent death.

## Approval Process

The Story Teller team will review the information and request with the Admins. If it meets the requirements (in-character discovery, severity of the situation, and reasonable justification for final death), we will open a ticket with the targeted player to inform them that a perma has been approved against their character.

From that point, the approved player may pursue and attempt to kill the target at their discretion.

## Rules of Engagement

- Only the players who received the approved perma may force the permanent death of the target.

- This does not apply during server-approved events such as a Blood Hunt or other mass hunt.

## Mutual Risk:

If you initiate combat with the intent to perma another player and they defeat you in that fight, they gain the immediate right to perma you in return during that same combat — no ticket required.

There is no immunity when you go for a perma. If you are hunting someone to end their story permanently, you are placing your own story at equal risk the moment you start the fight.

When you open a perma ticket we will ask for the following:

```text
- What is your characters name?
- What is your characters Race and Tribe/Clan if they have one?
- What is your characters age?
- Who are you wanting to perma?
- Why do you wish to pera them?
- What evidence do you have to back up your claims? Please note that screen shots and clips will hold much more weight than just giving us a typed out response. We want to see your evidence and how your character has come to know it.
```

## Reasons a player can be Perma Kill Approved:

- Kindred Clans: Lasombra, Salubri, Samedi, and Tzimisce\
- Kindred Sects: Autarkis and Sabbat\
- Garou: Black Spiral Dancers\
- Actions that betray your race or sect in ways that can be detrimental\
- Breaking the Masquerade/Lifting the Veil repetitively\
- For Kindred: trading or teaching disciplines like Thaumaturgy, Obtenebration, Necromancy, Quietus, Chimestry, Serpentis, and Vicissitude out of clan.\
- For Mystics: Being in the Technocratic Union or a Nephandi/Infernalist
