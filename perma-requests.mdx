## Permanent Character Death Guidelines

In the World of Darkness, actions have consequences. Player Kill or PK is the removal of a character from active play. This is permanent. Alternate “secret survival” stories or recreating the same character with minor changes are prohibited without staff approval. Everyone in this play setting is under soft perma guidelines. FAFO.

## Approval Process

Players seeking to PK another character’s story must open a Storyteller Ticket.

The Storyteller team reviews the request together with Admins. Approval is based on:

- Genuine in-character discovery\
- Severity of the situation\
- Reasonable justification for final death

If approved, staff will open a ticket with the targeted player informing them that a perma has been authorized against their character. From that point, the approved player(s) may pursue and attempt to kill the target at their discretion. Approval requires approval by at least two Storytellers and one Admin.

## Rules of Engagement

Only players who have received approval for that specific perma may force the permanent death of the target.

This restriction does not apply during server approved events (Blood Hunts, mass hunts, etc.).

## Mutual Risk & Combat Consent

If you initiate combat with the intent to perma another player and they defeat you, they gain the immediate right to perma you in return during that same combat. No additional ticket is required. By choosing to hunt someone to permanently end their story, you place your own story at equal risk the moment the fight begins. When stepping into a potentially lethal fight, players consent to the possibility of PK if their character suffers deadly damage. The risk is real and should never be ignored. The targeted player has every right to defend themselves and may kill their attacker in the process.\
## High-Risk Concepts (PK Clause)

Certain concepts carry inherent high risk due to their nature in the lore. These are more likely to warrant permanent death with proper justification and evidence:

## Kindred:

- Lasombra, Salubri, Samedi, Tzimisce\
- Autarkis, Sabbat\
- Caitiff, Thin-Blood

## Garou:

- Black Spiral Dancers\
- Stolen Moons 

## Other High-Risk Concepts

- Diablerists\
- Wights (0 Humanity)\
- Hunters (especially those with Pyromancy or True Faith)\
- Hunters wielding Artifacts (holy weapons, relics, etc.)\
- Mortals with Poisonous Blood to Kindred\
- Mortals who are “in the know” and actively share information or pose a threat\
- Mystics in the Technocratic Union, Nephandi, or Infernalists\
- Severe or repeated breaches of the Masquerade / Lifting the Veil\
- Trading or teaching out-of-clan Disciplines (Thaumaturgy, Obtenebration, Necromancy, Quietus, Chimerstry, Serpentis, Vicissitude, etc.)\
- Race or sect betrayal / treason in ways that are detrimental to your own people\
- Runaway Ghouls (under certain circumstances)\
- Sect betrayal, treason, or race traitor behavior\
- Unauthorized Diablerie\
- Repeated major IC rule violations (consistently violating barony rules, Traditions, etc.)\
- Long-term rivalries with significant buildup \
- Violation of another splat’s hub when discovered on the premises\
- Severe and repeated breaches of the Traditions/Masquerade

##  Invalid PK Reasons

These will not be approved:\
- Winning an argument or minor IC disagreements\
- Single insults or minor disrespect\
- Isolated feeding incidents with no escalation\
- Refusing romantic RP\
- Inactivity (without prior staff approval for a PKs\
- OOC grudges or bleed\
- FailRP revenge PKs\
- “Just because” or “they annoyed me”

## Consent PKs:

Outside of approved perma tickets and high-risk concepts, players may negotiate Consent PKs where boundaries and limits can be clearly defined in advance. Players do not need a ticket to document this and can do so among each other if both parties consent. We suggest using the consent system in game when the time comes to kill them just to protect all players involved.

Torture/Capture RP: Can occur with consent. PK is not authorized in these scenes unless it is consensual between players or approved via an appropriate perma ticket.

Staff reserves the right to deny any perma request that does not meet the standards of in-character justification, evidence quality, or overall server health and RP quality.

These rules exist to protect meaningful storytelling while allowing genuine high-stakes consequences. Play smart. Play in-character. And remember, when you reach for someone else’s final death, you are also reaching for your own.

## When you open a perma ticket we will ask for the following:

```text
- What is your characters name?
- What is your characters Race and Tribe/Clan if they have one?
- What is your characters age?
- Who are you wanting to perma?
- Why do you wish to pera them?
- What evidence do you have to back up your claims? Please note that screen shots and clips will hold much more weight than just giving us a typed out response. We want to see your evidence and how your character has come to know it.
```
