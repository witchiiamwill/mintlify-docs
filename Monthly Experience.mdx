<u>Monthly Experience:</u>\
\
Monthly experience is what will drive our characters to want to advance their characters and buy new things on their sheet.\
\
We should always be aiming to help players grow and evolve in roleplay.\
\
Max EXP each month is 10XP Baseline every month, with Justification of dots spent 4XP For ST Storylines Guide for ST Stories Aided in the story but 

\
- Played a Minor role - 1XP\
- Aided in the story but played a Slightly more Important role - 2XP\
- Help lead parts of the story and stayed throughout - 3XP\
- Became one of the story leaders and stayed for every scene - 4XP \
\

## Monthly EXP Questions:

- What is your Characters name?
- What is their Race, Age, and any tribe/clan affiliations?
- What has your character done or experienced this month?
- What ways do you feel your character has grown this month?
- How do you think your character has earned EXP month? Why?
- Have you participated in any Story Teller run lines? If so, what were they?
- How would you like to spend your experience this month?Please list out what you wish to take, the dot level, and cost. If you need help, a Story Teller can assist you with this.
- Please drop the old and new sheet with your proposed changes.
