---
title: "Fera Kinfolk Whitelist Questions"
---

```text
1) In your own words. What does it mean to be a Kinfolk?

2) Explain how the kinfolk for your character's specific Fera come to be. Are they born that way or chosen to be kinfolk?

3) Are Kinfolk of your character's Fera rare? Why or why not?

4) Explain in detail what being kinfolk to your specific breed of Fera means. How does your character's Fera interact with their Kinfolk?

5) How is being kinfolk to a Fera different from being Kinfolk to a Garou?

6) How does your character feel about the Fera and their place in that world?
Are they proud, resentful, fearful, loyal, conflicted, etc.? Explain why.

7) Describe the dynamics between the Fera your character is aligned with their kinfolk. What are the power dynamics? What are the expectations?

8) Describe your character’s specific relationship with the Fera the represent. Are they closely involved, distant, used as a resource, or something else?

9) What skills or value does your character bring to the Fera?
(Examples: social influence, medical knowledge, logistics, intelligence gathering, etc.)

10) Provide a detailed backstory for your character. Include upbringing, family ties, and how they became involved with the Fera.

11) How does your character handle fear and danger, knowing they are not a Fera? Give an example of how they might react in a threatening situation.

12) Kinfolk are often caught between the human world and Fera society. How does your character balance these two lives?

13) Describe a situation where your character might disagree with a Fera’s decision. How would they handle that conflict?
```
