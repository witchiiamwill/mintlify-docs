1) Explain the responsibilities of a Fostern or Adren within the Courts.
How are they expected to contribute compared to Cliath level Garou?

2) What are the laws of the Emerald Courts/Fera?
Describe its purpose and give at least two examples of how a Hakken might be expected to follow it in practice.

3) What is the Bushido Code? Why is it prevalent to the Hakken?

4) How does a Hakken earn respect among their peers?
What actions or behaviors would build, or damage, their reputation?

5) Describe the basic structure of the Courts

6) What is the Umbra, and why is it important to the Garou?
How experienced is your character with dealing with spirits or crossing into the Umbra?

7) Define Harano and Hauglosk in simple terms.
How might one of these affect your character over time?

8) Give an example of inter-tribal tension or difference in ideology.
How would your character respond when working with Garou from a tribe they don’t fully agree with?

9) Provide a backstory for your character.
Include key moments that helped them reach Fostern/Adren, along with any notable allies, rivals, or mistakes.

10) Describe your character’s personality and flaws.
What are they good at, and where do they struggle?

11) How does your character handle authority?
Do they follow orders without question, challenge leadership, or fall somewhere in between? Give an example.

12) Describe a difficult situation your character might face (combat, social, or spiritual).
How would they approach solving it?

13) Why should this character be approved at the level you are applying for (Fostern or Adren)?
What makes them more experienced than a Cliath?

14) Why is your character in San Andreas?
Hakken don't come here often. We wanna know why you did!
